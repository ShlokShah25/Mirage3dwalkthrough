Questions.

[ FAQ ]

Will my builder's brochure work?

Yes. A clear photo or screenshot of the floor plan is enough. Printed room sizes help Mirage get the scale right.

Do I need design skills?

No. Pick a style and every room is designed for you. Change anything afterwards by typing what you want.

Is it to scale?

Rooms are sized from the dimensions printed on your plan, so you can judge space before buying a sofa.

What's free?

Reading your plan, walking the bare 3D shell, and one room designed as a preview. After that, walking, moving things by hand and photos stay free.

Home Pass or Pro?

Designing your own home? Get a Home Pass. If you design homes for others, or more than one home a month, Pro works out cheaper.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

Your plan runs to the end of the month you paid for. After that your homes stay viewable and shareable; editing and new designs stop.
