Designs that fail

A design is only counted when it finishes and furniture is placed. If a design fails or you stop it, nothing is used, automatically.

Home Pass

You can ask for a full refund within 7 days of purchase if you have not designed a home with it. After a design is generated the Home Pass is non-refundable.

Subscriptions

Cancel Pro or Pro Max anytime from your account. Your plan runs until the end of the month you paid for and won't renew. We don't give partial refunds for unused days or allowances.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 working days.
